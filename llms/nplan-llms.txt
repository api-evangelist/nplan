# nPlan

> nPlan applies machine learning to construction project schedules to forecast and de-risk large capital projects. Trained on a large corpus of historical project schedules, its products analyze programme risk, benchmark schedules, and surface the interventions most likely to keep projects on time and on budget. nPlan is a private enterprise SaaS company backed by GV (Google Ventures); it exposes no public developer API at this time.

## About
- [Website](https://www.nplan.io): Forecast and de-risk construction projects with AI.
- [Careers](https://www.nplan.io/careers): Open roles at nPlan.

## Products
- nPlan Insights Pro: schedule risk analytics for construction programmes.
- nPlan Portfolio: portfolio-level project controls, with a Power BI integration.
